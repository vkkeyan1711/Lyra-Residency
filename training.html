Training 01

📞 Handling room price enquiries

Whenever a customer calls for room rates, first greet them politely. Ask when they need the room and how many rooms they require. Also clarify whether it is a marriage/group booking or an individual booking.

For marriage bookings, refer to the approved rate card before quoting the price.

Remember: Understand the customer's requirement first. Don't rush to quote a price!
Training 02

💰 Handling price bargaining

Our objective is to maximise occupancy, not to lose customers over small price differences. If a customer bargains for an AC room, try your best to accommodate them. If necessary, call management and get approval for a lower price.

Even during late-night enquiries, if rooms are available, reasonable price flexibility is allowed.

Remember: Never lose a genuine booking opportunity without trying!
Training 03

⭐ Getting Google reviews

Our business depends heavily on Google reviews. Every satisfied guest is a potential positive review.

Whenever a guest appreciates our service, politely request them to share their experience on Google. Make it easy by showing them the Google Review QR code.

Never force or repeatedly ask guests for reviews. Always provide good service first.

Remember: Today's happy guest can bring tomorrow's customer through a Google review!
