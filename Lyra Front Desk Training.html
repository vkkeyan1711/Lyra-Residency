Rate card

Keep these in your head. Never say a rate before you know the dates, the number of rooms, and the reason for the stay.

Always ask first: 1. Which dates? 2. How many rooms? 3. Marriage or bulk booking, or a normal single stay?
Very important: no key without full payment first. Say: "Sir, it is our hotel policy, for every guest."
